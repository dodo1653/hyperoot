      What it is

      [[BRAND]] launches coins on pump.fun. Every coin launched here trades against $[[TICKER]] instead of SOL, using pump.fun's own support for a coin as the pair (a "pump-coin quote"). $[[TICKER]] itself trades against [[QUOTE]]. Everything happens in pump.fun's programs; [[BRAND]] adds no contract of its own.

      Launching

      1. Connect a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare or Backpack).
      2. Add a picture (saved as a 512 px JPEG), a name, a ticker (case kept, up to 10 letters or digits), a description and links (X, website, Telegram).
      3. Pick your creator fee: 0.5, 1, 2 or 3 % of every trade.
      4. Approve one transaction. It creates the coin on pump.fun, paired with $[[TICKER]], and sets the fee split in the same transaction.

      There is no buy at launch (no dev buy). Name, ticker, links and picture are fixed for good once the coin exists.

      The $[[TICKER]] pair

      A coin here is priced in $[[TICKER]]. Buying it means paying in $[[TICKER]], so every buy of any coin here first buys $[[TICKER]]; wallets and terminals that support pump.fun's multi-hop route do this in one go. The chain is: your coin → $[[TICKER]] → [[QUOTE]].

      Fees and the split

      Each coin's creator fee is paid in $[[TICKER]] on every buy and sell. pump.fun's own fee-sharing program splits it on chain, set in the launch transaction: [[CREATOR]] % to the creator and [[BURN]] % to the [[BRAND]] buyback wallet. Payouts go straight from pump.fun to both; nothing passes through [[BRAND]]'s hands first.

      pump.fun makes the creator the admin of their fee split, and it offers no supported way to lock it, so a creator could change it later. Every coin's split is readable on chain.

      Burns

      Fees on these coins arrive in $[[TICKER]], so the buyback wallet's share is $[[TICKER]] already: no swap. Every 10 minutes a keeper calls pump.fun's distribute instruction for each coin (which pays the creator their [[CREATOR]] % too) and burns everything the buyback wallet holds with a token burn. Each burn shows on this site with its Solana transaction.

      The CA ending

      Every coin launched here has a contract address ending in [[SUFFIX]]. The addresses are ground ahead of time on our relay: a key whose address ends in those letters signs its coin's create once, after your wallet, and then pump.fun owns the mint. A copycat can reuse a name, not the ending.

      Risks

      Memecoins are extremely risky: most go to zero, prices move in seconds, and anyone can launch anything. Nothing here is financial advice. [[BRAND]] is independent and not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by pump.fun or Hyperliquid.

      FAQ

      Is this pump.fun?

      No. Coins are created in pump.fun's program, trade on its curve and show in its feed, but [[BRAND]] is an independent site.

      Can I search any coin?

      Yes: paste any pump.fun CA in the search. Coins launched here open with their live numbers; other coins show their pair, market cap and creator fee.

      Why can't I buy at launch?

      The launch transaction already holds the create, the fee split and its accounts; there is no room, and there is no dev buy by design.

      Where do my creator fees go?

      [[CREATOR]] % straight to your wallet in $[[TICKER]], paid out by pump.fun's distribute instruction.
